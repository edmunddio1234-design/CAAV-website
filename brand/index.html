Two brands, one family.

A full brand package for the CAAV and one for Immersion in Motion, built on a shared palette and type set.

Read this first. These two packages are proposed. We have not seen the CAAV's current logo, and Southern University has not approved any name, logo or photo use. Nothing here replaces an existing logo until the CAAV board and Southern University say so in writing. The marks are original drawings made for this site.

Package 1

The CAAV brand package

1. Logo suite

Primary lockup, on white
Reversed mark, on navy
Mark only, on aged paper

The mark is a corner of a CAVE: two walls and a floor. Navy is the ceiling, blue the left wall, gold the floor.

2. Rules

Do
  • Write "the CAAV" in text. Write the full name once: Campus Alliance for Advanced Visualization.
  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the cube on every side. Smallest size: 24 px high for the mark, 120 px wide for the lockup.
  • Use navy text on gold, white text on navy, ink text on white.
  • Use gold as a highlight behind one phrase, not a page color.
Do not
  • Recolor the cube or swap its faces.
  • Put the mark on a busy photo without a navy or white panel.
  • Use gold text on white. It is 2.6:1, too faint to read.
  • Add shadows, glows or gradients.

4. Voice

Plain words and short sentences. Say what a thing does. Skip: delve, testament, seamless, revolutionize, game-changer, unlock, elevate, leverage, robust, cutting-edge, empower. Headline sizes are big and bold, body is calm and gray-black.

Package 2

The Immersion in Motion brand package

1. Logo suite

Primary lockup, on white
Truck mark, on navy
Truck mark, on aged paper

The mark is a simple box truck with gold speed lines. It is original and does not copy any Southern University mark, vehicle wrap or logo.

2. Rules

Do
  • Write "Immersion in Motion" with the descriptor "Southern University Mobile CAVE" on first use.
  • On the truck: logo large on the side, gold stripe, navy lower band, sponsor logos in an equal row, all the same height.
  • Show people using it. People are the subject, not the equipment.
  • Show diverse learners: racially mixed, mixed ages, men and women.
Do not
  • Use the Southern University logo, wordmark or seal without written permission.
  • Imply a result no study supports. Say "up to" and name the PwC study with its limits.
  • Quote sponsor prices before Southern gives them.
  • Put readable text on a truck in AI-made images.

Shared system

One palette, one type set.

Colors

Ink#121212
Text, borders. 18.7:1 on white
Paper#FFFFFF
Page background
Aged paper#F1F1F1
Cards, panels
Faded ink#E1E1E1
Hairlines, dividers
Navy#19264A
Bars, dark sections. 14.8:1 on white
Navy 2#243560
Hover on navy
Blue#3C62A0
Accents, links on white. 6.1:1
Gold#CC9633
Highlight, buttons. Navy text on gold 5.6:1

Type

  • Archivo 800Headlines, tight tracking (-0.04em at the largest size)
  • Public Sans 400/500Body and buttons, 17 px body, 1.6 line height
  • Azeret MonoLabels, dates, captions in capitals
  • KalamOne handwritten line per page at most

All four are open-license (OFL) fonts, safe on public websites. Licenses are in /assets/fonts.

Shape and motion

Corners 20 px for cards, 28 px for navigation pills, 40 px for buttons. Motion is a short rise-in from GSAP, switched off for people who ask their device for reduced motion. Touch targets are at least 44 px.
