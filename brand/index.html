Nothing here replaces an existing logo until the CAAV board and Southern University say so in writing. The marks are original drawings made for this site.

Package 1

The CAAV brand package

1. Logo suite

Primary lockup, on white
Reversed mark, on ink
Mark only, on page gray

The mark is a corner of a CAVE: two walls and a floor. The three faces keep the colors drawn in the logo files; the website around them stays black, white and gray.

2. Rules

Do
  • Write "the CAAV" in text. Write the full name once: Campus Alliance for Advanced Visualization.
  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the cube on every side. Smallest size: 24 px high for the mark, 120 px wide for the lockup.
  • Put the mark on white, page gray, or ink. Use the reversed mark on ink.
  • Let the mark be the only color on the page. Text stays ink or gray.
Do not
  • Recolor the cube or swap its faces.
  • Put the mark on a busy photo without an ink or white panel.
  • Set body text in the light gray used for headline first lines. It is for large text only.
  • Add shadows, glows or gradients.

4. Voice

Package 2

The Immersion in Motion brand package

1. Logo suite

Primary lockup, on white
Truck mark, on ink
Truck mark, on page gray

The mark is a simple box truck with speed lines. It is original and does not copy any Southern University mark, vehicle wrap or logo.

2. Rules

Do
  • Write "Immersion in Motion" with the descriptor "Southern University Mobile CAVE" on first use.
  • On the truck: logo large on the side, a stripe and lower band in the mark's colors, sponsor logos in an equal row, all the same height.
  • Show people using it. People are the subject, not the equipment.
  • Show diverse learners: racially mixed, mixed ages, men and women.
Do not
  • Use the Southern University logo, wordmark or seal without written permission.
  • Imply a result no study supports. Say "up to" and name the PwC study with its limits.
  • Quote sponsor prices before Southern gives them.
  • Put readable text on a truck in AI-made images.

Shared system

One palette, one type set.

Panels and cards

Text, dark panels. 18:1 on page gray

Secondary text, captions. 5.8:1 on page gray
Muted#8A8A8A
First line of two-tone headlines. Large text only (3.1:1)
Hairline#DCDCDC
Dividers and borders

Type

Supreme 300/400 · display

Visualization

Switzer 400/500 · body and UI labels

Campus Alliance for Advanced Visualization
  • Supreme 300/400Headlines, up to 104 px, line height 1.0, tracking -0.04em. Two-tone: first part in muted gray, the rest in ink.
  • Switzer 400/500Body at 17 px, 1.55 line height. Navigation, buttons and captions in small capitals-only labels, 12 to 14 px.

Both are free fonts from the Fontshare (Indian Type Foundry) library, self-hosted, licensed under the ITF Free Font License. The license is in /assets/fonts/FFL.txt.

Shape and motion

Corners 24 px for cards and panels, 10 px for buttons (no pill buttons), 40 px soft organic edges on large image blocks. Motion is GSAP: headlines rise in, images scale in on scroll and tilt slightly under the pointer. All of it is switched off for people who ask their device for reduced motion. Touch targets are at least 44 px.
