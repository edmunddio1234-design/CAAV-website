Southern University · Brand sheet · October 2026

XR Jags

Extended Reality Jaguars Research Group: name, wordmark, colors, type and rules

Brand

XR Jags and Southern University colors.

The look of this site after the October 2026 rebrand: Southern University gold, navy and Columbia Blue on a calm gray-and-white base. Print this page to make the brand PDF.

1 · Name

XR Jags / Extended Reality Jaguars Research Group

  • Short nameXR Jags. Write it this way in text and headings.
  • Full nameXR Jags / Extended Reality Jaguars Research Group. Use once, on first mention.
  • One-line descriptionThe student research group for extended reality at Southern University and A&M College, Baton Rouge.
  • TaglineExplore, Innovate, and Discover with XR Jags.
  • Earlier namesVR Jags (student site). This web address was the CAAV conference site until October 2026.

4 · Color rules

Do
  • Use SU Gold for the main action on a page and for highlights.
  • Put navy (#00263A) text and icons on gold.
  • Use #8A6A00 when gold-colored text sits on white or gray.
  • Use Columbia Blue as the second accent: links, hover and active states, focus rings, small labels, the reading-progress line and bands.
  • Keep the gold top-section field only at the top of a page.
Do not
  • Use orange, terracotta or red-orange anywhere. Anything that was orange is now SU Gold.
  • Put white text on gold.
  • Use Columbia Blue for something that was orange.
  • Recolor the Southern University logo.

5 · Type

Supreme 300/400 · display

XR Jags

Switzer 400/500 · body and UI labels

Extended Reality Jaguars Research Group

Both are free fonts from the Fontshare (Indian Type Foundry) library, self-hosted, licensed under the ITF Free Font License. The license is in /assets/fonts/FFL.txt.

Shape and motion

Corners 24 px for cards and panels, 10 px for buttons (no pill buttons). Motion is GSAP: headlines rise in, images scale in on scroll. All of it is switched off for people who ask their device for reduced motion. Touch targets are at least 44 px.
