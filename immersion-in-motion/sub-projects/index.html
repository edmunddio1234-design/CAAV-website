Sub-projects

Seven sub-projects, one truck.

Each application domain on the truck is a sub-project with its own aligned program.

SP-01 · K12-EDU

K-12 education and mobile STEAM outreach

Aligned program. Southern University Reaching Across the Digital Divide (SURADD)

Truck domain. K-12 education

SP-02 · ICLERT-CRISIS

Crisis management and emergency preparedness

Aligned program. Immersive Crisis Leadership and Emergency Response Training (ICLERT)

Truck domain. Emergency preparedness, crisis management

SP-03 · OPH-HEALTH

Healthcare technology and public health VR

Aligned program. Louisiana Office of Public Health (OPH) VR Project

Truck domain. Healthcare

SP-04 · CAAV-RES

Exploratory research and advanced visualization

Aligned program. CAAV National Visualization Network

Truck domain. Exploratory research

SP-05 · ADV-MFG

Manufacturing technologies and cyber-additive XR

Aligned program. Enhancing Additive Manufacturing Education with Cybersecurity and VR

Truck domain. Manufacturing technologies

See the proposed AM Lab pathway

SP-06 · UAV-DRONE

Drone exploration and autonomous aerial systems

Aligned program. Reality Capture, Photogrammetry and BVLOS Simulation

Truck domain. Drone exploration

SP-07 · CAVE-OPS

Student-led Mobile CAVE systems integration

Aligned program. Vehicle engineering, multi-wall projection calibration, statewide tour deployment

Truck domain. Vehicle and tour operations

Still to come. Scope boundaries, technical stacks, deliverables, leads and milestone dates for each sub-project are not on this page yet. They are added when the project framework file is supplied.
