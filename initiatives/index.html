K-12 outreach

VR in middle school classrooms.

Through SURADD, XR Jags brought VR headsets, standards-aligned VR science modules and teacher training to classrooms within a 15-mile radius of Southern University, and mobile VR systems to rural communities.

Read the SURADD projectBook the Mobile CAVE for a school

CAVE tours

See the CAVE on campus.

CAVE tours on campus last about one hour, for visitors ages 12 and up, with no more than six people per group (from the student site). For larger groups or visits off campus, ask for the Mobile CAVE truck.
