Ratana Prinyawiwatkul

Computer Science Instructor

ratana.warren@sus.edu

LinkedIn profile of Ratana Prinyawiwatkul
Read bio

Dr. Ratana Prinyawiwatkul Warren is an esteemed instructor in the Department of Computer Science at Southern University and A & M College. Under the NTIA, she collaborates with Dr. Brian Warren to lead the management of CAVE, maintaining its hardware and software, and developing applications using Unity and C#. With over a decade of teaching experience, she brings extensive knowledge and expertise to her role. Before joining Southern University, Dr. Warren was a programmer at Windrock Inc., Knoxville, TN. She is a professional member of the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) and holds numerous certificates, including Cybersecurity Academy Orientation, Cybersecurity Foundation, Enterprise Design Thinking Practitioner, IBM Data Science Practitioner, and Data Science Tools. Dr. Warren has completed Unity training courses, equipping her with foundational skills in Unity and the ability to create interactive, real-time 3D projects. Her portfolio includes several Unity projects, ready for professional application. Her academic contributions include publications such as "Multimodal Course Design and Implementation Using LEML and LMS For Instructional Alignment" in the International Journal of Computer Science & Information Technology (IJCSIT), and "Investigating Wireless and Internet of Things Technologies Security Threats and Attacks" in the International Journal of Wireless & Mobile Networks (IJWMN), Vol. 14, No. 3, June 2022.

Sri Divya Reddy Mettu is a doctoral researcher, grant writer, and developer specializing in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Virtual Reality (VR), Additive Manufacturing (AM), and emerging learning technologies for experiential education. A Full-Stack Java Developer with industry experience and skilled in Python and Unity, she integrates technical innovation with educational research to advance STEM education, workforce training, and digital inclusion.

At Southern University and A&M College, Sri leads the IMPACT (Industrial Modeling, Prototyping, and Computational Testing) Lab, focusing on engineering design and additive manufacturing. Sri also contributes to the OPH and ICLERT projects, advancing immersive training for healthcare and emergency-response professionals.
