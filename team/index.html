Brian C. Warren

XR Jags Research Group Leader and Principal Investigator, FUEL

LinkedIn profile of Brian C. Warren
Read bio

Dr. Brian C. Warren is an experienced industrial project and maintenance manager utilizing talents for companies such as NASA, BWXTY-12, and Galata Chemicals. In addition, he has experience as a research coordinator and administrator for university projects and collaborative partnerships.

Brian has provided funding from industry to Southern University A&M College for over $500K in contracts and grants from agencies such as the US Department of Energy and other agencies. He managed to support the establishment of mentor-protégé agreements between Southern University, ORNL, and BWXT Y-12. The coordination between academia and industry has been the focus of his endeavors in establishing a relationship with the private sector. Brian has set the stage for creating relationships with industry and academia. He has mentored students on a continuous basis and seeks to ensure that they are successful in the workforce.

Brian has been involved in and hosted several conferences and seminars that engage all entities of business and academia. Brian was featured as the keynote speaker on several occasions and has worked to involve the community in many research feedback efforts, yielding academic findings.

Deidre Hardy Street

Executive Director for Outreach and Research Engagement and XR Jags Student Organization Advisor. Co-Principal Investigator, SURADD

LinkedIn profile of Deidre Hardy Street
Read bio

Mrs. Deidre Hardy Street is an accomplished STEM educator, higher education administrator, and grant development specialist with more than 30 years of leadership in research, program management, and strategic project development.

Throughout her distinguished career spanning K-12 and higher education, Mrs. Street has played a pivotal role in writing, securing, and managing in excess of $50M in federal awards. Her grant portfolio encompasses funding from leading federal agencies including ED, EDA, NASA, NSF, NTIA, and ONR. Honored with the Southern University Grants Award (SUGA) for obtaining a million-dollar award within a single grant cycle, she leverages her technical expertise and oversight to build institutional capacity, helping faculty and students develop competitive grantsmanship in STEM/STEAM research.

A dedicated philomath committed to innovative pedagogy, Mrs. Street works with XR Jags and Southern University to guide learners of all ages through new technological frontiers and avenues of discovery.
