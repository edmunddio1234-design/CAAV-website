About

We are XR Jags.

XR Jags / Extended Reality Jaguars Research Group is the student research group for virtual, augmented and mixed reality at Southern University and A&M College, Baton Rouge.

01 · Who we are

A dynamic organization at Southern University.

We are the VR Jags at Southern University and A&M College - Baton Rouge, a dynamic organization dedicated to advancing VR technology while delivering tangible, real-world results.

We bring together ambitious students who are passionate about developing cutting-edge VR applications, turning creative ideas into fully functional, market-ready products. The group now goes by XR Jags (Extended Reality Jaguars), because the work covers virtual, augmented and mixed reality.

02 · What we do

We provide our services to schools, contractors, businesses, organizations, and individuals.

XR Application Development

Bridging the physical and digital world to build next generation immersive experiences.

3D Printing & Rapid Prototyping

Turning digital concepts into physical reality with unprecedented speed and precision.

Reality Capture

Digitizing the physical environment to create high-fidelity, 3D models of the real world.

Workforce Training & Development

Empowering professionals with the evolving skills needed to thrive in a tech-driven workplace.

Request services

03 · How we work

Hands-on, project-based, professional.

Our goal is to provide a professional environment where members not only learn and innovate but also create VR solutions that have real commercial potential.

With a focus on hands-on experience and project-based learning, we expect our members to deliver high-quality results through collaborative work, workshops, and industry-grade tools.

04 · Where we work

On campus, and on the road.

Based in P.B.S. Pinchback Hall on the Southern University campus. Once it launches, the Southern University Mobile CAVE takes the work to schools and communities.

  • Courseware StudioThe central workspace where the team develops and tests models for the CAVE environment, with computers, VR headsets and charging stations.
  • 3D Printing LaboratoryRapid prototyping and fabrication, with 3D printers, modeling workstations and post-processing tools.
  • CAVE (Cave Automatic Virtual Environment)Surrounds users with high-resolution, 3D projections on multiple walls, for data visualization, simulation and real-time interaction.
  • Leica RTC360 3D Laser ScannerReality capture equipment listed by the team.
  • Artec 3D Handheld ScannerReality capture equipment listed by the team.
  • Stratasys Fortus 380mc 3D Production System and Dremel DigiLab 3D Printer3D printing equipment listed by the team.
