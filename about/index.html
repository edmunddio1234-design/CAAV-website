About

A volunteer-driven nonprofit for visualization people.

The CAAV describes itself as an information resource for higher education worldwide.

Focus

Its focus is immersive visualization platforms, advanced video screen formats, advanced visualization software, and the sustainability and operation of high-tech visualization facilities.

Five points

The charter

  1. Serve as a unified information resource for researchers, teaching faculty and staff who work with advanced visualization technology.
  2. Act as a clearinghouse for collaboration across K-12, undergraduate, graduate, postdoctoral and other higher education research, corporate services and teaching that use advanced visualization.
  3. Champion the adoption of more advanced visualization hardware and software.
  4. Operate as a volunteer-based organization that encourages academic-style open discussion among campus representatives.
  5. Support professional development, mentoring and public outreach, including student demonstrations and competitions.
