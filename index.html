About XR Jags

Who We Are

The next generation of engineers, educators, and leaders.

Through hands-on projects and professional workshops, we strive to deliver results that go beyond the screen. If you're ready to build XR solutions with real-world potential, you’ve come to the right place.

Services

What We Do

Four services, for schools, contractors, businesses and you.

01

XR Application Development

Bridging the physical and digital world to build next generation immersive experiences.

02

3D Printing & Rapid Prototyping

Turning digital concepts into physical reality with unprecedented speed and precision.

03

Reality Capture

Digitizing the physical environment to create high-fidelity, 3D models of the real world.

04

Workforce Training & Development

Empowering professionals with the evolving skills needed to thrive in a tech-driven workplace.

Projects

Our Work

Explore our current and previous projects.

  • Southern University Reaching Across the Digital Divide (SURADD)A VR curriculum for middle school students, funded by the National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA).
  • Immersive Crisis Leadership and Emergency Response Training (ICLERT)VR disaster training for crisis managers and first responders, with the Louisiana Department of Health.
  • Louisiana Office of Public Health VR ProjectVR and tablet anatomy and health physics simulations across the Southern University System.
  • Enhancing Additive Manufacturing Education with Cybersecurity and VRA VR-infused 3D printing and cybersecurity curriculum, supported by the NSF IUSE:EHR program.
  • Immersion in Motion: the Southern University Mobile CAVEA CAVE on a truck that brings 3D demonstrations to schools, plants, hospitals and agencies.
