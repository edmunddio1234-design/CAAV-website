What the CAAV does

Five points from the charter.

One information resource

Serve researchers, teaching faculty and staff who work with advanced visualization technology.

A clearinghouse

Connect collaboration across K-12, undergraduate, graduate, postdoctoral, corporate services and teaching.

Better tools

Champion the adoption of more advanced visualization hardware and software.

Open, volunteer-run

Encourage academic-style open discussion among campus representatives.

People first

Support professional development, mentoring and public outreach, including student demonstrations and competitions.

Ways to take part

Talk, learn, compete, meet.

Member Slack

A channel for questions and discussion between people who run facilities.

Monthly connect day

On the 10th of each month more members are online to connect.

Annual conference

CAAVCon: presentations, panels and informal networking. Past conferences and videos are archived.

Student AR/VR Design Competition

A competition for students working in augmented and virtual reality.
